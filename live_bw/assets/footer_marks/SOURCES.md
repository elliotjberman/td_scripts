# Footer marks

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-24:

- [FCC](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FCC_New_Logo.svg): Federal Communications Commission; vector by Rehua. Commons lists it as public domain, US federal government work.
- [CE](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Conformit%C3%A9_Europ%C3%A9enne_(logo).svg): MatthiasDD. Commons lists it as public-domain simple geometry/text.
- [UL](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UL_Mark.svg): Underwriters Laboratories. Commons lists it as public-domain simple geometry/text.
- [Crossed-out bin / WEEE](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WEEE_symbol_vectors.svg): European Union; vector adaptation by Mateozlatar. Commons lists it as public-domain common-property information.

Original SVG files are retained beside white variants. The UL inner circle is
converted to a transparent knockout. The WEEE variant omits the lower date bar
to match the supplied reference. The combined white strip uses 54-pixel marks,
16-pixel gaps, and a 90-pixel-high canvas. Its PNG is rendered at four times the
design resolution, then embedded as a locked TOP in the portable TD component.
The SVG TOP is deprecated in the installed TouchDesigner version.
